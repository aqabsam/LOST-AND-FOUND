About Us

LOST AND FOUND

"Lost and Found" is a dedicated platform designed to help individuals recover misplaced belongings efficiently and securely. Whether you've lost an item or found something someone else may be missing, our site enables you to upload details such as the item's description, location, and time of loss or discovery. When entries match, both parties are connected to facilitate the return. Our goal is to foster a responsible, community-driven approach to lost property management.
